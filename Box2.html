Box 2

A simple, recyclable container dish perfect for hiding, storing, or dramatically emerging from.

Ingredients

Main Structure:

  • Layers of thick paper pulp (a.k.a. cardboard)
  • 1 corrugated middle layer (for strength and crunch)
  • 2 flat liner sheets (top and bottom support)
  • Industrial glue (to hold everything together)
  • Optional: packing tape for reinforcement

Directions

  1. Start by creating the corrugated layer—this wavy middle part adds durability and shock absorption.
  2. Attach flat paper layers on both sides to form a sturdy board.
  3. Cut and crease the board into a foldable pattern.
  4. Secure the edges using glue or tape.
  5. Leave the top flaps open for easy human insertion (optional but recommended for meme use).

Serving Suggestions

  • Use for shipping items safely (absorbs impact and protects contents an absulute box)
  • Perfect for storage, moving, or hiding inside dramatically.
  • Can be stacked easily for maximum efficiency.
  • Secure the edges using glue or tape.
  • Best paired with: one suspicious man inside.

Tips

Keep dry—water weakens cardboard and ruins the structure, Avoid storing food or valuables long-term (it attracts pests and moisture issues). For best meme results: choose a box slightly too small for comfort

Bonus Variation

    Stealth Box Mode

    Add a human inside and remain completely still

    → increases invisibility by +100
